## Result
- `MIN_DF_DEFAULT = 0.005` and `MAX_DF_DEFAULT = 0.99` in `config.py`, passed to scikit-learn's `CountVectorizer` as proportions: a term is removed if it occurs in fewer than 0.5% or in more than 99% of the documents. `DF_THRESHOLDS` and `get_df_auto` are removed. Claude Code's research agent had also proposed a floor of two documents; it has no source and was not adopted.
- Advanced settings: two percentage fields ("Remove rare words: in fewer than this % of documents", default 0.5; "Remove common words: in more than this % of documents", default 99) replace the `min_df` and `max_df` fields and the "Auto-adjust by corpus size" checkbox. A message appears if the rare-word share is not lower than the common-word share, and the analysis stops with a specific message if no words remain. The authors approved the wording of the two help texts (4 November 2026).
- `build_dtm` reports terms removed as rare and as common, their share of the vocabulary, the share of word occurrences removed, the document counts behind the two thresholds and the documents left with no words. The Overview shows them; `metrics.json` carries them.
- Svevo, *Una vita*, first 20,000 words (21 documents), checked with Streamlit's AppTest: 0.5% of 21 documents is less than one document, so no rare term is removed; no term occurs in all 21 documents.
